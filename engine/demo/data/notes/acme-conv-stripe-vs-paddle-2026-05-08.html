Payment Provider Decision ? Stripe over Paddle

We decided to chose Stripe over Paddle as our payment processor. Key factors: Stripe has a more mature webhook reliability story with configurable retry policies, event logging in the dashboard, and signature verification. Paddle's merchant-of-record model would have simplified EU VAT compliance but removes control over checkout UX customization.

Our team also had existing Stripe integrations from previous projects, reducing onboarding time. The Stripe TypeScript SDK is well-maintained and ESM-compatible, matching our stack.

Updated src/payments/stripe.ts to wire the Stripe SDK and updated src/config.ts to add stripeSecretKey and stripeWebhookSecret as required environment variables.
