Subscription Cancellation ? Graceful by Default

We decided that subscription cancellation should default to cancel_at_period_end rather than immediate cancellation. Users who cancel should retain access until the end of the period they have already paid for ? this is both fair to customers and reduces churn-related support requests.

Immediate cancellation is reserved for admin actions (e.g., fraud or TOS violations) and requires the admin role to invoke via the DELETE /subscriptions/:id?immediate=true endpoint. Updated src/payments/stripe.ts and src/api/routes.ts to enforce this role check.
